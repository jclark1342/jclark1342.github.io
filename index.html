Forms of Work

Material ways in

The portfolio treats product design as a loop between hand drawing, digital modeling, photographic observation, and sculptural making.

Drawings

Scans, process boards, ideation pages, construction notes, and proportion studies that map decisions before the object is fixed.

3D Models

CAD forms, render boards, Rhino and SolidWorks explorations, and digital studies used to test volume, assembly, and surface.

Photography

Documentation, darkroom-style image work, fabrication photos, and visual studies that frame objects in context.

Sculptural

Objects, vessels, furniture, soft prototypes, and form experiments where the piece is understood through weight, texture, and presence.
